# xiaoke.ai 2026-11-05 Release Notes

[简体中文](./release-20261105.md) · **English**

## She Has Your Week of Entertainment Fully Booked

It has been a little over a week since the 2026-10-28 A package, and xiaoke.ai has learned quite a few new tricks.

She used to chat, dance, and watch movies with you. Now she can join a table of bluffing cards, respond to livestream chat and gifts, launch the game you want to play, and bring SillyTavern character cards onto the 3D stage. Watch-together has also grown beyond “take a look and say something”—she can now follow continuous video as it unfolds.

**In short: she can play, watch, host, and step into more of your favorite characters.**

## Phantom Tavern: Take a Seat—No Need to Tell the Truth Yet

Introducing **Phantom Tavern**, a four-seat bluffing card game.

- Play against three AI opponents that choose whether to follow or challenge based on their own goals and the state of the table.
- A three-chamber revolver, scoring, and complete follow-or-challenge turns.
- Four card-art styles, cinematic judgments, and victory/defeat presentations.
- Characters can talk without freezing the entire table.
- Assign different voices to different table characters.

She can join the performance, but the model does not invent the rules: cards, scoring, and judgments remain code-controlled.

## Live Interaction: Chat, Follows, and Gifts Have Arrived

New livestream interaction for **Douyin and Bilibili**.

- Receive chat, follows, and gifts; aggregate related messages and deduplicate reconnects.
- Schedule responses by message age and queue capacity so a gift flood does not completely drown out ordinary viewers.
- Generation is not delivery: a response counts as delivered only after the stage confirms actual playback.
- A separate local rehearsal lab provides scripted viewers, real or substitute models, product voices, and the 3D stage.
- Practice duplicate delivery, source disconnects, model failures, and playback failures, with persistent receipts and recovery of unfinished gift responses.

Rehearse locally before asking a real audience to test her improvisational skills.

> The rehearsal lab verifies the local pipeline. Authentication, webpage protocol changes, rate limits, and long-running stability on real platforms still need separate testing with your target livestream room. This is not a commercial-production SLA guarantee.

## Continuous Video Companionship: She Can Follow the Plot Now

The local **MiniCPM-V 4.7 1B** eye has graduated from screenshots to continuous native video understanding.

- Continuously capture a sliding video window with real timestamps.
- Avoid queues of obsolete observations and release resources automatically when watching stops or becomes idle.
- Dedicated scene context, subtitle timelines, and language awareness for movies, short videos, and music.
- Game understanding can combine League client state with local OCR.
- Tell the difference between “take a look at this error” and “watch this match with me,” without leaving vision running after every one-time look.
- Video and sound remain on your computer; she does not click, operate, or play for you.

Before: “Pause for a second and let me look.” Now: “Keep playing—I’m following along.”

## SillyTavern Character Cards: Bring Favorite Characters onto the Stage

Welcome to the **SillyTavern character-card ecosystem**.

- Import v1/v2/v3 PNG and JSON cards, plus AICC cards.
- Export SillyTavern v2 PNG cards.
- Preserve alternate greetings, creator metadata, and unknown extension fields from the original card.
- Lorebooks support keywords, regular expressions, constant and selective entries, injection order and position, and bounded recursion.
- Add an optional user persona without rewriting the character to explain who you are.

Character-card formats and the Lorebook concept come from the open SillyTavern ecosystem. xiaoke.ai provides compatible import, layered-prompt integration, and 3D-stage presentation. Card authors retain their copyrights.

## Natural-Language Game Launching: Say the Title, Not the Installation Tutorial

Ask her to open an installed game from **WeGame, Steam, or Epic**.

- Resolve the local game library and select a title using your current intent and context.
- Check the process after launch instead of claiming that a sent command means the game is already open.
- If the game is unavailable, open only its platform—never install it automatically.
- Discussing, rejecting, or hypothetically mentioning a game does not launch it.

You can say “I want to play that one,” or “play that one with me.” She can launch the game first, then prepare to watch together.

## Voice and Performance: More Voices, Less Talking Over the Game

- Added **Xiaomi MiMo-V2.5-TTS**, with nine preset voices and emotion directions.
- Improved sentence splitting and short-clip playback, so long replies are less likely to become one unwieldy audio block and tavern speech does not block play.
- Ordinary chat, rewriting, tarot, welcomes, follow-ups, goodbyes, touch, Code companion, and watch-together now share one unified dsh character session.
- Clearer handling for interruptions, corrections, and stale responses prevents an old answer from leaking into the next turn.
- Local Qwen TTS returns to the verified official dependency combination, fixing a compatibility branch that loaded successfully but could generate incorrect speech.

## Desktop and Update Experience

- Share a configurable logo and color theme across the app, window, tray, and installer.
- Check release information through both **GitHub and Gitee**.
- A vision-process logging switch makes watch-together troubleshooting easier.
- Updated window controls and community entries, with QQ-group and WeChat QR images included in the desktop resources.
- Completed vision-runtime and packaging integration, reducing cases where a feature works from source but loses components in the packaged app.

## Before You Upgrade

- These notes cover the **2026-11-05 A package**, including changes made after the 2026-10-28 A package.
- When using separate agent A/B packs, download the combination provided on the release page and keep the A/B timestamps identical. Do not mix different batches.
- You still need your own chat, speech, and livestream credentials; they are not bundled with the app.
- Continuous local video and offline speech can use significant GPU and memory resources. On lower-spec machines, disable watch-together or choose an online speech provider.
